Capstone: CamVision AI – Intelligent CCTV Monitoring

A final-year industry capstone focused on designing an AI-powered monitoring system for commercial CCTV networks, with emphasis on cloud architecture, alert workflows, and edge-to-cloud system planning.

View Public Artifacts Back to Projects
Overview

What this project involved

As part of my final-year capstone project, my team collaborated with an industry partner to design a system for monitoring the performance and quality of commercial CCTV cameras. The goal was to architect an AI-powered solution that could detect issues such as blur, tampering, and downtime in real time, then trigger alerts or maintenance actions through a connected cloud workflow.

Project Status

Current phase

Phase 1 focused on planning, architecture definition, tool setup, and technical validation. This included system design, workflow modelling, project coordination, and evaluating the feasibility of on-site AI detection with cloud-based metadata handling.

My Role
